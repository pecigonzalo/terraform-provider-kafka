```release-note:new-resource
`kafka_quota` manages the quotas of one `user`, `client_id` or `ip` entity. An entity can be the default entity. The limits are `producer_byte_rate`, `consumer_byte_rate`, `request_percentage`, `controller_mutation_rate` and `connection_creation_rate`. Kafka 2.6 or later is necessary. To import, use an id such as `user=alice,client-id=billing`, `ip=10.0.0.1` or `user=<default>`. (#178)
```
